# Story sources

The story text in `src/shared/pusaka.ts`, `src/shared/figures.ts` (role, story)
and `scripts/generate-bank.ts` (titles, scenes, tales) follows these sources.
Scenes the game invents (Cakil fighting Bima, the punakawan following Bima,
Bagong retelling the battle, the Kurawa court plotting) are written as the
standard parts a dalang adds to any lakon: the perang kembang (flower battle),
the goro-goro (clown interlude) and the court scene.

Spellings used throughout: Pandawa, Kurawa, Astina, Amarta, Durna, Dewaruci,
Candramuka, Tirta Pawitra, Nemburnawa, Begawan Ciptoning, Niwatakawaca,
Supraba, Pasopati, Hanoman, Alengka, Argasoka, Maliawan, Sayempraba, Sempati,
Wibisana, Rujakpolo. The figure name is now "Dewaruci" too.

## What was corrected

Pusaka

- `wijayakusuma`: owner `arjuna` → `''`. It is Kresna's heirloom, which revives the dead (Kresna is not one of our puppets).
- `astagina`: owner `hanoman` → `''`. Batara Surya gave it to Dewi Windradi, and it caused the curse on Anjani, Subali and Sugriwa.
- `tirta`: renamed "Tirta Prawitasari" → "Tirta Pawitra" (the form in the scholarly sources; also tirta perwita).
- `guwawijaya`: renamed "Busur Guwawijaya" → "Busur Rama". No source could be found for the name Guwawijaya. Also dropped "only he can string it": in the Ramayana that is Siwa's bow, which Rama breaks to win Sinta.
- `ampal`: renamed "Cincin Ampal" → "Cincin Rama". No source for "Ampal".
- `gandewa`: dropped the invented "left with the twins". It now gives the Mahabharata origin (Agni, Varuna, Khandawa).
- `konde`: now a hair jewel (Valmiki's cudamani) and no longer a hairpin as fact.
- `pancanaka`: no longer says it can "split a mountain giant". It is now the nail that kills the naga in Dewaruci.
- `kentongan`, `kendhi`: now say that the link to Petruk/Bagong is the game's own, not tradition.
- `dadu`, `gada-inten`, `kalimasada`, `blencong`, `kayon`, `pasopati`, `obor`, `mahkota`, `poleng`, `rujakpolo`, `pulanggeni`, `keris-cakil`: rewritten with sourced facts. Each has a new `source` field.

Figures

- Semar: "elder god" → Batara Ismaya, brother of Batara Guru (texts differ on who is elder).
- Gareng: added his origin as Bambang Sukodadi.
- Petruk: role "The long-nosed son" → "Semar's tall son". "King for a day" is now the lakon Petruk Dadi Ratu.
- Bagong: added how he was made from Semar's shadow.
- Yudhistira: "never once told a lie" → "patient, honest". The Mahabharata has his half-truth about Aswatama.
- Nakula: dropped "healer" (not found). Madri → Madrim (wayang spelling).
- Sengkuni: role is now "Patih of Astina".
- Batara Guru: dropped "Suralaya" (sources disagree on the name of his heaven). Added Nandini and the Kirata test.
- Dewaruci: role "thumb-sized" → "tiny god in the sea". He is the size of Bima's palm, with Bima's face, and Bima enters his _left ear_.
- Cakil: he is a Javanese invention. He dies "often" (not always) by his own keris.
- Rahwana: "unkillable while his spell holds" → "his magic arts keep him from dying" (Pancasona/Rawarontek).

Story weeks

- Week 1: The mountain is now Candramuka (Reksamuka appears only in one source). Indra and Bayu are now freed from a curse; before, they "rose up" as new gods. The second errand to the sea now comes from Durna, and the Kurawa court only hopes he drowns. Added the naga Nemburnawa and the left ear. Day 5 is staged as the goro-goro.
- Week 2: Added Mpu Kanwa, the seven nymphs (Supraba, Tilotama), Indra as the old brahmin and Niwatakawaca. Batara Guru now appears as the hunter. Added Supraba learning the giant king's secret and the arrow through the tip of his tongue.
- Week 3: Added Jatayu, the camp at Maliawan, Sugriwa, Sayempraba and Sempati, Indrajit's capture and Wibisana's exile. Sinta's token is a jewel from her hair. She refuses to be carried home. The last tale leads back to Semar opening week 1.

## Not verified (left out or flagged)

- The name **Mamangmurka** for the boar. No source was found, so the boar is unnamed. The kakawin calls the demon Muka.
- **Guwawijaya** and **Ampal** as names of Rama's bow and ring.
- A sea giant's name on Hanoman's crossing (Javanese versions name Wilkataksini, but only a satirical source was found). It stays "the sea giant".
- Whether a specific Javanese telling calls Sinta's token a tusuk konde. The pusaka keeps that name, but the story only claims a hair jewel.
- **Prawitasari** as a name of the water. It is replaced by the sourced Tirta Pawitra / perwita.
