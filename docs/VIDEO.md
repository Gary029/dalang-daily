private window so the warm-up plays. Keep the sound on: the gamelan carries
the edit. Captions in quotes are on-screen text, not voice-over (judges often

## How to get each shot quickly (browser demo)

- Warm-up: `game.html` in a private window.
- Thursday / Saturday: `game.html?warm=done&day=3` / `day=5`.
- Sunday (two lamps, finale button on the result card): `game.html?warm=done&day=6`.
- A full book and the builder: `game.html?warm=done&album=all`.

Export at 1080×1920 (vertical) for the phone shots, or 1920×1080 with the
phone recording centred and the kawung pattern behind it. Upload to YouTube
(unlisted is fine) and paste the link into `docs/DEVPOST.md`.
